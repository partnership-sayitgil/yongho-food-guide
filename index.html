01Rice & stew🍚

Korean set meals

Oryukdo Gamasotbap

오륙도가마솥밥

A Korean meal built around pot-cooked rice. Choose a soft tofu stew set or a fermented soybean stew set.

ON THE MENU순두부 + 가마솥밥 정식Soft tofu stew & pot-cooked rice set

Cheonggukjang (청국장) is a fermented soybean stew with a strong, distinctive aroma.

Busan pork soup

합천국밥집

Try dwaeji-gukbap, Busan’s pork soup with rice. This restaurant is known for its clear broth and also serves boiled pork slices.

ON THE MENU돼지국밥 / 수육Pork soup with rice / boiled pork slices

A pork-based meal. Check which cuts are included when ordering.

03Noodle stop🍜

Korean noodle soup

Somunnan Kalguksu

소문난칼국수

A local kalguksu restaurant. Kalguksu means knife-cut wheat noodles, served in a bowl of hot broth.

ON THE MENU칼국수Knife-cut noodle soup

Ask about the broth ingredients if you avoid fish or meat. Wheat noodles are not gluten-free.

04Something sweet🍧

Korean dessert

Yongho-dong Halmae Patbingsu

용호동할매팥빙수단팥죽 본점

A red-bean dessert stop: cool down with patbingsu (shaved ice topped with sweet red beans), or choose a warm bowl of sweet red-bean porridge.

ON THE MENU팥빙수 / 단팥죽Red-bean shaved ice / sweet red-bean porridge

The tourism listing mentions milk among its ingredients. Ask about dairy and other allergens.

A few words go a long way

Show this when you order.

이 메뉴 하나 주세요.One of this, please. (Point to the menu.)

이 음식은 매운가요?Is this dish spicy?

영어 메뉴가 있나요?Do you have an English menu?

여기로 가 주세요.Please take me here. (Show the address.)

Menus and opening hours can change. Check with the restaurant before travelling. If you have allergies or dietary requirements, confirm ingredients with the staff; this guide does not certify any venue as vegetarian, vegan or halal.
