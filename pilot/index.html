01Rice & stew🍚

Korean set meals

Oryukdo Gamasotbap

오륙도가마솥밥

A Korean meal built around pot-cooked rice. Choose a soft tofu stew set or a fermented soybean stew set.

ON THE MENU순두부 + 가마솥밥 정식Soft tofu stew & pot-cooked rice set

Cheonggukjang (청국장) is a fermented soybean stew with a strong, distinctive aroma.

02A Busan classic🥣

Busan pork soup

Hapcheon Gukbapjip

합천국밥집

Try dwaeji-gukbap, Busan’s pork soup with rice. This restaurant is known for its clear broth and also serves boiled pork slices.

ON THE MENU돼지국밥 / 수육Pork soup with rice / boiled pork slices

A pork-based meal. Check which cuts are included when ordering.

03Noodle stop🍜

Korean noodle soup

Somunnan Kalguksu

소문난칼국수

A local kalguksu restaurant. Kalguksu means knife-cut wheat noodles, served in a bowl of hot broth.

ON THE MENU칼국수Knife-cut noodle soup

Ask about the broth ingredients if you avoid fish or meat. Wheat noodles are not gluten-free.

04Something sweet🍧

Korean dessert

Yongho-dong Halmae Patbingsu

용호동할매팥빙수단팥죽 본점

A red-bean dessert stop: cool down with patbingsu (shaved ice topped with sweet red beans), or choose a warm bowl of sweet red-bean porridge.

ON THE MENU팥빙수 / 단팥죽Red-bean shaved ice / sweet red-bean porridge

The tourism listing mentions milk among its ingredients. Ask about dairy and other allergens.

06Beef BBQ🥩

Korean beef barbecue

Igane Myeongin Galbisal

이가네명인갈비살 부산용호점

A beef barbecue restaurant in Yongho-dong. Choose beef rib meat without marinade, with a fruit-based marinade, or with a soy-sauce marinade.

ON THE MENU생갈비살 한판 / 과일양념한판 / 간장양념한판Beef rib meat: unseasoned (400g), fruit-marinated (500g), or soy-marinated (400g or more)Each platter: ₩34,900 (Naver listing, 7 Oct 2026)

Group seating and reservations are listed. The restaurant lists one hour of parking support at the adjacent Yangyang parking lot; confirm when you arrive.

Places to go

A short taxi or bus ride away.

Taxi about 7 min (≈₩4,800) to Dongsaengmal

Igidae Coastal Walk

이기대 해안산책로

Rocky cliffs, a suspension bridge and wide views of Gwangan Bridge along the sea.

The full trail takes about 2.5 hours. Try the short Dongsaengmal section after a meal; wear trainers.

Google Maps ↗ · Naver Map ↗

Taxi about 9 min (≈₩5,500)

Oryukdo Skywalk & Sunrise Park

오륙도 스카이워크 · 해맞이공원

A short glass-floored walkway over the sea, with coastal views and a hillside park next to it.

May close in strong wind or bad weather. Shoe covers are provided.

Taxi about 7 min (≈₩4,800)

Yongho Starlight Park

용호별빛공원

A seaside park for an easy stroll, with sunset and night views of Gwangan Bridge.

It can be windy by the water.

유엔기념공원

A memorial site: please keep quiet and follow the signs.

A few words go a long way

Show this when you order.

이 메뉴 하나 주세요.One of this, please. (Point to the menu.)

이 음식은 매운가요?Is this dish spicy?

영어 메뉴가 있나요?Do you have an English menu?

여기로 가 주세요.Please take me here. (Show the address.)

Menus and opening hours can change. Check with the restaurant before travelling. If you have allergies or dietary requirements, confirm ingredients with the staff; this guide does not certify any venue as vegetarian, vegan or halal.
